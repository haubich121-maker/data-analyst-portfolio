# Business Insights — Fintech Loan Performance Analysis

## 1. Executive Finding

The business experienced a significant increase in loan applications following marketing expansion.

However, total disbursed value declined.

This indicates that the main business challenge was not a lack of application volume, but a deterioration in application quality and downstream conversion.

---

## 2. Key Business Findings

### Finding 1 — Application volume increased, but application quality declined

Loan applications increased by approximately 35%.

At the same time, the average credit score decreased from 685 to 634.

This suggests that the additional application volume was associated with a weaker customer risk profile.

### Business Implication

Increasing acquisition volume alone does not necessarily translate into higher disbursement.

The business should evaluate acquisition quality together with downstream loan performance.

---

### Finding 2 — Approval performance weakened among higher-risk segments

The analysis shows weaker approval performance across higher-risk customer segments, particularly Risk C, D and E.

Risk E decreased from 29.87% to 21.17%.

### Business Implication

A larger proportion of applications from higher-risk customers can increase application volume while reducing the percentage of applications that successfully progress toward approval and disbursement.

---

### Finding 3 — Rejection reasons indicate application-quality issues

The main rejection reasons identified in the analysis include:

- Low Credit Score
- High Debt-to-Income
- Income Not Verified
- Incomplete Profile
- Policy Rule Hit

### Business Implication

The business should not focus only on generating more applications.

Improving the quality and completeness of applications could have a stronger impact on approval and disbursement performance.

---

### Finding 4 — Acquisition performance should be evaluated beyond applications

A channel or campaign that generates a large number of applications does not necessarily generate high disbursed value.

Therefore, acquisition performance should be evaluated across the full funnel:

Applications → Approval → Disbursement

### Business Implication

Marketing investment should increasingly focus on channels and campaigns that generate successful downstream outcomes rather than application volume alone.

---

# 3. Root Cause Analysis

The performance gap can be summarized into three major drivers:

### 1. Customer Mix

Marketing expansion increased application volume but also brought in customers with weaker credit profiles.

### 2. Application Quality

A meaningful proportion of applications were associated with low credit scores, high DTI, unverified income or incomplete profiles.

### 3. Funnel Conversion

Higher application volume did not translate proportionally into approval and disbursement.

Therefore:

**More Applications ≠ More Disbursement**

---

# 4. Business Recommendations

## Recommendation 1 — Shift from Volume-Based to Quality-Based Acquisition

Instead of optimizing marketing campaigns only for application volume, monitor:

- Application Volume
- Approval Rate
- Disbursement Rate
- Disbursed Value
- Average Disbursed Amount

This allows the business to identify acquisition sources that generate higher-quality customers.

---

## Recommendation 2 — Review High-Risk Acquisition Sources

Identify channels and campaigns with:

- High Risk C–E share
- Low Approval Rate
- Low Disbursement Rate
- High rejection rate

These sources should be reviewed before increasing marketing spend.

---

## Recommendation 3 — Improve Application Quality

Reduce avoidable rejection by improving:

- Income verification
- Customer profile completeness
- Credit-quality screening
- Debt-to-income assessment

The objective is to improve the quality of applications entering the approval funnel.

---

## Recommendation 4 — Allocate Marketing Budget Based on Downstream Performance

Marketing budget should be evaluated using the complete business funnel:

**Marketing → Applications → Approval → Disbursement**

The preferred channels should be those that demonstrate strong downstream conversion and disbursed value, rather than simply generating the largest number of applications.

# 7. Conclusion

The analysis suggests that the decline in disbursed value was not primarily caused by insufficient application volume.

Instead, the key issue was the relationship between:

**Application Growth → Customer Quality → Approval → Disbursement**

Therefore, the business should prioritize **high-quality acquisition and downstream conversion** rather than optimizing solely for application volume.
