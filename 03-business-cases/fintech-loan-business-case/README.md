# Fintech Loan Performance — Business Case

## 1. Business Context

A fintech lending business expanded its marketing activities and experienced significant growth in loan applications.

However, the increase in application volume was accompanied by changes in customer credit quality, rejection rates, and post-approval conversion.

The business needs to understand what is driving these changes and where management should focus its efforts.

---

## 2. Business Problem

### Key Question

> **Why did loan applications increase significantly while downstream loan outcomes did not improve at the same pace?**

The analysis aims to answer four key questions:

1. Is the increase in applications coming from higher-quality customers?
2. Has the applicant risk profile changed?
3. Why are more applications being rejected?
4. Are approved applications successfully converting into disbursements?
