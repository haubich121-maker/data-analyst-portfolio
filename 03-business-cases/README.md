# Business Cases

This section presents business-oriented data analysis cases focused on identifying business problems, diagnosing potential root causes, and translating data findings into actionable recommendations.

Unlike the technical projects in this portfolio, these cases focus more on **business thinking, analytical reasoning, and decision-making**.

---

## 01. Fintech Loan Performance Business Case

### Business Context

A fintech lending business expanded its marketing activities and experienced significant growth in loan applications.

However, the growth in application volume was not accompanied by a similar improvement in downstream loan outcomes.

This raised a key business question:

> **Why are loan applications increasing while loan performance is not improving at the same pace?**

---

### Business Problem

The business needs to understand:

- Why application volume increased
- Whether customer quality changed
- Whether credit risk deteriorated
- Why more applications were rejected
- Whether approved applications were successfully converted into disbursements
- Which acquisition channels may be contributing to the change
- What actions should be prioritized
